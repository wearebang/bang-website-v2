About Us

BANG began in Harlesden in 1999. What started with young people, creativity and community radio has grown into a connected ecosystem for youth leadership, enterprise, research, organisational development and housing.

Our Mission

BANG is a values-led ecosystem connecting youth leadership, enterprise, research, organisational development and housing. Rooted in Brent and connected across London and beyond, we create practical routes for young people to lead, build skills, shape decisions and access opportunities.

Our History

Founding

BANG began in Harlesden in November 1999, started by Jennifer Ogole OBE, who had begun as a young leader in Brent and continues to live on the Stonebridge Estate, with a simple principle that has stayed in place ever since: young people already have talent, intelligence, creativity and the ability to lead, and BANG’s role is to create the relationships, resources, responsibility and opportunities through which that leadership can grow. Media and creativity gave young people their first platform, with Life FM community radio beginning in 2001 and BANG Radio launching in 2007, and until 2015 BANG Radio developed DJs and presenters who went on to careers across the UK media industry before the station became The Beat London.

BANG is a values-led ecosystem connecting youth leadership, enterprise, research, organisational development and housing. Rooted in Brent and connected across London and beyond, we create practical routes for young people to lead, build skills, shape decisions and access opportunities.
